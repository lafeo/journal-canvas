Features

Everything stays in your files

No database. No cloud sync. No account. Works with plain text you already own.

⬡
Live knowledge graph
A D3 force simulation clusters nodes by connection strength. The more two entities share documents, the closer they sit.
◈
AI entity extraction
Claude reads each document and extracts people, places, events, themes, and concepts. Existing entities are recognised and merged, never duplicated.
📖
Inline reader
Click any node to read every document it appears in. Every entity in the text is colour-highlighted. Click a name to focus the graph on it.
✎
Click-to-edit
Click anywhere on a document in the reader to edit it. Saves on blur, then re-analyses and updates the graph automatically.
✦
Themes & concepts sidebar
Themes and concepts live in a dedicated sidebar. Click one to immediately illuminate everything connected to it across the graph.
☾
Light & dark mode
Toggle between a warm dark canvas and a clean light paper mode. Preference is saved between sessions.
How it works

From folder to graph in seconds

1
Point it at your notes folder
Any folder of .md or .txt files works. Journal Canvas watches for changes in real time.
2
Claude extracts structured knowledge
Each document is sent to Claude with your existing entity list. Claude identifies entities and reuses names it has already seen — no duplicates.
3
The graph builds itself
Entities that co-appear in documents are linked. The force simulation settles the graph into natural clusters.
4
Explore, read, and edit
Click nodes to focus connections. Open the reader to see every mention of an entity across all your notes. Edit inline — changes update the graph automatically.
