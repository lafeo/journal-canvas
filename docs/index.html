Features

Everything lives in your files

No proprietary database. No sync service. Journal Canvas works with plain text files you already own.

⬡
Live knowledge graph
A force-directed graph that clusters nodes by how many documents they share — not by type. Heavily connected entities pull together naturally.
◈
AI entity extraction
Claude reads each document and extracts people, places, events, themes, and concepts. Existing entities are recognised and merged, never duplicated.
📖
Inline reader
Click any node to read every document it appears in. Every entity in the text is colour-highlighted. Click a name to focus the graph on it.
✎
Click-to-edit
Click anywhere on a document in the reader to edit it. Saves automatically on blur, then re-analyses and updates the graph.
✦
Theme & concept sidebar
Themes and concepts live in a dedicated sidebar. Click one to instantly illuminate everything connected to it across the graph.
◌
Hide without deleting
Hide any note from the graph without touching the file. Its entities vanish from the graph. Restore in one click whenever you need them back.
How it works

From files to graph in seconds

1
Point it at your notes folder
Any folder of .md or .txt files works. Journal Canvas watches the folder for changes in real time.
2
Claude extracts structured knowledge
Each document is sent to Claude with your existing entity list. Claude identifies people, places, events, themes, concepts — and reuses names it has already seen.
3
The graph builds itself
Entities that co-appear in documents are linked. A D3 force simulation settles the graph into natural clusters. The more two entities appear together, the closer they sit.
4
Explore, read, and edit
Click nodes to focus their connections. Open the reader to see every mention of an entity across all your notes. Edit inline — changes update the graph automatically.
